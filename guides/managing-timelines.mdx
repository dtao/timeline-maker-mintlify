Timeline Maker lets you maintain as many separate timelines as you need, each stored independently in your browser. The collapsible left sidebar is your control centre for switching between projects, creating new ones, and keeping your workspace tidy. Every change you make is auto-saved, so switching timelines never risks losing your work.

## The Sidebar

The sidebar lists every timeline you have saved. The header displays the total count of saved timelines at a glance. Click any timeline name in the list to switch to it immediately — your current work saves automatically before the switch.

To collapse the sidebar and reclaim screen space, click the `<` arrow button in the sidebar header. When the sidebar is collapsed, the button changes to `>` — click it to expand the sidebar again.

<Note>
  All timeline data is saved automatically to browser localStorage as you work. You do not need to press a save button — switching timelines, closing the tab, or reloading the page all preserve your data.
</Note>

## Create a New Timeline

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sidebar">
    If the sidebar is collapsed, click the `>` arrow button to expand it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click + New">
    Click the `+ New` button in the sidebar footer. A fresh, empty timeline is created and immediately becomes the active timeline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your timeline">
    Click the timeline name field in the main header area — it is an editable input. Type a descriptive name and press Enter or click away to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your first milestone">
    Your new timeline is ready. Use the `+ Add milestone` button in the control panel or the bottom of the milestone list to start building it out.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rename a Timeline

Click directly on the timeline name displayed in the main header area. The name becomes an editable input field. Type the new name and press Enter or click elsewhere on the page to save the change. The updated name appears in the sidebar immediately.

## Duplicate a Timeline

Select the timeline you want to copy by clicking its name in the sidebar. Then click the `Duplicate` button. A complete copy of the timeline — including all milestones, dates, owners, statuses, and risks — is created and added to the sidebar. The duplicate becomes the active timeline so you can start editing it straight away.

<Tip>
  Duplication is a fast way to create a new project from an existing template. Build a baseline timeline once, then duplicate it for each new project or quarter.
</Tip>

## Delete a Timeline

Select the timeline you want to remove by clicking its name in the sidebar. Click the `Delete` button. A confirmation prompt appears — confirm to permanently remove the timeline and all its data.

<Warning>
  Deletion is permanent. Timeline Maker does not have a recycle bin or undo for deleted timelines. Once you confirm the deletion, the timeline and all its milestone data are gone.
</Warning>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordion title="How do I rename a timeline?">
  Click the timeline name in the main header area — the editable name input field sits at the top of the canvas. Click it, type the new name, and press Enter or click away to save. You do not need to open any menu or dialog.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I recover a deleted timeline?">
  You cannot. Deletion is permanent and there is no undo, no recycle bin, and no recovery mechanism. If you think you might want a timeline later, duplicate it before deleting the original, or export it as an SVG or PNG for reference.
</Accordion>
