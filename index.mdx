## What you can do with Timeline Maker

Timeline Maker gives you an interactive, drag-and-drop timeline view alongside a structured list editor. Every change you make is saved automatically to your browser, so your work is always there when you come back.

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Timeline Maker">
    Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No server, no install, no sign-up required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first milestone">
    Click **+ Add milestone** in the control panel or directly on the timeline axis to place a milestone at a specific date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add team members and risks">
    Use the owner picker to assign milestones to people. Open the milestone dialog to attach risks and track their status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export your timeline">
    Click **Download SVG** or **Download PNG** to save a clean, shareable version of your timeline.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  All your data is saved in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a server. See [Data & Storage](/reference/data-storage) for details.
</Note>
