Timeline Maker is a lightweight milestone planner that runs entirely in your browser. Open `index.html`, and you have a fully interactive timeline ready to use — no installation, no sign-up, and no data leaving your machine. Everything you create is stored locally in your browser, making it a fast, private tool for project managers, team leads, and anyone who needs to communicate a plan visually.

## What you can do

Timeline Maker gives you a complete planning workspace in a single HTML file. Here is what it covers:

**Multiple timelines.** A collapsible left sidebar lets you create, duplicate, rename, and delete as many saved timelines as you need — each stored independently in your browser.

**Interactive SVG visualization.** Your milestones render as a clean SVG timeline. Drag a marker to reschedule it, click a title to rename it inline, or hover over any milestone to reveal quick edit and delete controls.

**Milestone management.** A list editor below the timeline gives you a structured view with columns for Milestone, Date, Owner, Status, Signal, and Actions. Milestones carry one of four statuses — Planned, In Progress, Completed, or Overdue — each with a distinct color so the state of your plan is visible at a glance.

**Risk tracking.** Attach risks directly to milestones. Each risk has its own owner, description, and update history, and can be marked Open or Resolved so nothing slips through the cracks.

**Shared people list.** Maintain a single roster of team members — with names, email addresses for Gravatar images, or uploaded avatar photos — and assign them as owners across milestones and risks.

**SVG and PNG export.** Download your timeline as a vector SVG for editing or a clean PNG for sharing. Exported images strip all edit controls so what stakeholders see is exactly the plan.

**Browser-local storage.** All data is saved automatically to `localStorage` under the key `timeline-maker-state-v1`. No account, no server, no sync — your data stays on your device.
